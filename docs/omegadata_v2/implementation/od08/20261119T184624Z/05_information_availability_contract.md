# Information availability
AvailabilityObservation retains economic, observed, published, known and ingested times, completed interval, exact artifact/record, listing, units/bases, evidence and version. Unknown required availability or incomplete intervals block. Publication, observed time and completed interval may not exceed cutoff. Ingestion is not substituted for publication or known time.

STRICT uses EvidenceAuthority and the unchanged source-rights register. Conservative treatment is a separate policy requiring an explicit bound, reviewed semantics and independently authorized policy evidence under STRICT. Caller-provided delays/references and canaries cannot self-authorize it. No default delay.

Intraday decisions cannot use eventual daily close/volume or future adjustment information. Zero-valued anonymous observations are software bookkeeping controls, not market observations or financial-accuracy evidence.

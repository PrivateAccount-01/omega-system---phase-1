# Consumer policies remain separate
MEMBERSHIP returns source-selected accounting without strategy filters. RESEARCH adds declared observation/quality/adjustment requirements. LIQUIDITY applies a versioned window and thresholds. EXECUTION consumes bound raw-basis OD07 execution eligibility.

No universal financial threshold is invented. Research exclusions do not alter official membership. Later unavailable observations or future history cannot decide earlier eligibility. PROVISIONAL_ONLY remains provisional regardless of confidence. Financial and execution acceptance flags remain false pending separate authentic-source/release gates.

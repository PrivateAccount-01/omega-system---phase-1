# Coverage is independent from selected accounting
UniverseCoverage records exact scope, method, source artifact, independent reference, valid/known bounds, expected identities/count, snapshot/transition classification and authority. States are COMPLETE_VERIFIED, COMPLETE_FOR_DECLARED_NON_MARKET_SCOPE, PARTIAL, PROVISIONAL, UNKNOWN, CONFLICTING and SOURCE_BLOCKED.

A SNAPSHOT covers one explicit date only. Interval coverage needs independently declared starting/ending sets, an ending reference and exact intervening ADD/REMOVE transitions bound to revisions and boundaries. Missing transitions remain PARTIAL; incorrect endpoints, identities or duplicates conflict. No indefinite forward-fill.

COMPLETE_VERIFIED cannot come from non-market controls. Actual authentic coverage remains BLOCKED_REAL_DATA. Empty-with-no-coverage is UNKNOWN, not proof of absence. Empty controlled coverage cannot confer financial acceptance. Two snapshots alone do not prove complete change history.

# OmegaData OD-05U: Owner Real-Reference Pack Checklist

**Purpose:** Provide the smallest genuinely authentic and lawfully usable NSE Capital Market bhavcopy evidence pack for first real financial verification. **This form does not confer approval.** Fields must reflect real observed facts, never be generated or backdated by an agent.

## Submit four separate private components

1. **Publisher documentation:** Original dated NSE CM format/circular/annexure version that actually applies to the nominated data date. Original saved bytes if permitted, original official URL, independently captured SHA-256 and length, retrieval evidence and exact format/version applicability.
2. **A genuine real sample:** One existing original NSE CM market ZIP/CSV for a specified date, obtained with rights covering local research/analysis. Keep bytes private and outside Git. The local manifest/URL/name alone is insufficient proof of exchange origin.
3. **Independent expected fields:** A separate, independently reviewed literal row table covering row ordinals; symbols, series, ISIN, dates, price strings and exact decimals, volume/units, columns and identities; prepared without using the Omega parser's output as its sole reference.
4. **Permission and pin record:** Named owner/reviewer, actual review timestamp, each original SHA/size and authenticated receipt chain; granular allowed operations (document reading, research parsing, storage, derived analysis, trading use, sharing/publication). Unknown means no permission.
